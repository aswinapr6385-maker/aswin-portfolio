I am a Software Test Engineer with 1+ year of hands-on experience in manual testing of enterprise-style web applications in a fintech/client-driven environment.

My primary expertise lies in conducting thorough functional, regression, integration, UAT, and end-to-end testing across multi-tiered financial applications. I specialise in validating intricate business workflows—including merchant management, settlement calculations, payouts, and MDR operations—ensuring zero data leakage and high system integrity.

Engineering Initiative: To augment my professional manual testing background, I have developed comprehensive self-driven automation projects utilizing Playwright with Java, Selenium WebDriver, TestNG, Page Object Model (POM), Maven, Git, and Jenkins CI/CD.
